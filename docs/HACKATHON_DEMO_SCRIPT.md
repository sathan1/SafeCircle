# SafeCircle — 3 to 5 Minute Hackathon Demo Script

## Hook & Introduction (30 seconds)
*"Good morning, judges. Imagine an 18-year-old college student traveling back to her hostel late in the evening. Her mother naturally wants to know she's safe.*

*In today's world, she has only two bad choices:*
1. *Either surrender her absolute privacy 24/7 by sharing her continuous live GPS location,*
2. *Or turn off location sharing entirely and remain completely unmonitored during an emergency.*

*SafeCircle solves this dilemma forever with **Privacy-First Progressive Emergency Protection**.*

*The core thesis:*
> **Traditional tracking asks: Where is she?**  
> **SafeCircle asks: What information has she chosen to share, and when?**

---

## Step-by-Step Live Two-Phone Walkthrough (3.5 minutes)

### Device Setup
- **Phone A (Left Hand)**: The Student's phone running SafeCircle.
- **Phone B (Right Hand / Projected)**: Her Mother's phone displaying the Trusted Contact view.

---

### Step 1: Pre-Configured Privacy Control (Phone A)
- **Show Screen**: Open **Privacy Policy** on Phone A.
- **Script**:  
  *"Before leaving campus, she configures who can see what. Look at this clear banner:*  
  **'You control what each person can see.'**  
  *For Mom, she authorizes status in normal conditions, approximate area during caution, and live GPS only if things escalate to crisis. Her friends receive even less."*

---

### Step 2: Starting a Safe Journey (Phone A & Phone B)
- **Action**: Tap **Start Safe Journey** from College Campus to Home. Select SafePath route.
- **Show Screen**: Show Phone A in **NORMAL** (Green) state. Show Phone B.
- **Script**:  
  *"Her journey begins. Now look at Phone B — her mother's phone.*  
  *Does Mom see her exact live GPS coordinates? **No.**  
  Mom only sees that a journey is active and on schedule. Her daughter's daily autonomy is preserved."*

---

### Step 3: Explainable Route Deviation (Phone A)
- **Action**: In Demo Simulator on Phone A, tap **[Route Deviation]** (+25 pts).
- **Show Screen**: Phone A turns **CAUTION** (Amber). A prompt appears: *"Are you okay? 60s countdown"*.
- **Script**:  
  *"Her vehicle takes an unlit detour. SafeCircle's explainable signal engine detects the variance and immediately asks her: 'Are you okay?'*  
  *Notice: SafeCircle does NOT cry wolf or call 911 immediately."*

---

### Step 4: No Response → ELEVATED Escalation (Phone A & Phone B)
- **Action**: Tap **[Simulate Missed Check-In]** (+25 pts).
- **Show Screen**: Phone A transitions to **ELEVATED** (Orange, Score 50). Phone B auto-updates in 2.5s.
- **Script**:  
  *"She doesn't respond. SafeCircle escalates to ELEVATED.*  
  *Now look at Phone B! Within two seconds, Mom's phone updates automatically over cellular data.*  
  *What does Mom see now? She sees an alert notice and an approximate area — but still no intrusive continuous tracking, exactly as pre-authorized."*

---

### Step 5: Critical Emergency Escalation (Phone A & Phone B)
- **Action**: On Phone A, press and hold the red **EMERGENCY SOS** button for 2 seconds.
- **Show Screen**: Phone A enters **CRISIS** (Red). Phone B unlocks live GPS and route trajectory.
- **Script**:  
  *"The situation escalates. She activates Emergency SOS with a deliberate 2-second hold to prevent accidental pocket taps.*  
  *Instantly, the backend unlocks her live GPS coordinates and situational evidence specifically for designated emergency contacts.*  
  *Mom can now see her live location and call for assistance."*

---

### Step 6: Graceful De-Escalation & Privacy Restoration
- **Action**: On Phone A, tap **"I Am Safe · De-escalate to Normal"**.
- **Show Screen**: Phone A returns to **NORMAL**. Phone B immediately strips live coordinates.
- **Script**:  
  *"Once safe, she taps 'I Am Safe'. Her score resets to zero, and the backend immediately revokes live GPS access from all contacts.*  
  *Privacy is dynamically restored."*

---

### Step 7: Realistic Failure Modes (Phone Loss, GPS Loss, Offline)
- **Action**: In Demo Simulator, tap **[Phone Unavailable]** and show **Offline Mode**.
- **Show Screen**: Show Wearable Fallback banner and Offline Banner.
- **Script**:  
  *"We don't make false claims. If her phone battery dies or the phone is lost, SafeCircle demonstrates companion wearable fallback.*  
  *If she loses cellular signal, SafeCircle enters Offline Mode, preserving local safety rules and timestamping cached telemetry so no one is deceived by fake live data."*

---

## Conclusion & Closing Statement (30 seconds)
*"Judges, personal safety should empower women — not subject them to digital surveillance.*

*SafeCircle proves that deterministic safety rules, explainable signals, and progressive disclosure can protect women in genuine emergencies while respecting their dignity and freedom every single day.*

*Thank you. We welcome your questions."*
